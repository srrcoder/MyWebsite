Frequently Asked Questions

How do I apply?

Apply online via our website by selecting a course and completing the application form. Contact admissions for help with documents and deadlines.

What documents are required?

Typically: high school/previous degree transcripts, identity proof, passport-size photo, and any certificates relevant to your course. Specific courses may have extra requirements.

Do you offer scholarships?

Yes — merit and need-based scholarships are available. Contact our scholarships office or admissions to learn eligibility and application process.
